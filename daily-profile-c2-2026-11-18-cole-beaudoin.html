Profile

Beaudoin is a hard, direct two-way center who plays through traffic, wins inside ice and brings a forecheck-and-matchup identity. His game is built on force, motor and habits more than flash.

The development focus is carrying his OHL offense into pro hockey with more pace and quicker decisions against older defenders. His ceiling is a middle-six center; the safer path is a hard bottom-six center who can pressure pucks and help on the penalty kill.
