Biography and Rangers acquisition

The Rangers selected #10 Benjamin MacBeath 64th overall in the second round of the 2026 NHL Draft. The Calgary, Alberta native is a left-shot defenseman now developing at the University of Denver.

MacBeath posted 7-44-51 in 67 WHL regular-season games for the Calgary Hitmen in 2025-26. A mobile two-way defender, he uses skating, edge work and transition instincts to support the attack. Adding strength, simplifying puck decisions under pressure and translating those offensive instincts against older NCAA competition are the next priorities.
