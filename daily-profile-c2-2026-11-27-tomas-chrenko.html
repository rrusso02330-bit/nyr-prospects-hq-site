Biography and Rangers acquisition

The Rangers selected #9 Tomas Chrenko 81st overall in the third round of the 2026 NHL Draft. The Nitra, Slovakia native is a 5-foot-11, 172-pound right-shot center.

Chrenko produced at the senior level for HK Nitra before the draft, posting 9-22-31 in 44 regular-season games in 2025-26 and adding 2-10-12 in 19 playoff games as Nitra won the title. A creative center, he makes plays with touch, pace and vision; adding strength and rounding out his defensive detail are the next developmental priorities.
