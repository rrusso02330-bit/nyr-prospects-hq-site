Profile

Greentree is a big, skilled scoring winger whose profile is driven by puck protection, a heavy shot, net-front touch and the patience to find passing lanes when defenders overplay his release. He produced 119 OHL points in 2024-25 before following with 74 in 52 regular-season games for Windsor last season.

The development focus is pace and defensive detail as the scoring tools meet professional pressure. His most natural role is a middle-six scoring winger who can work below the dots and provide power-play value.
