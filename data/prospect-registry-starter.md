# NYR Prospects HQ - Prospect Registry Starter

Research date: 2026-06-20

This is the first working pass at the Rangers prospect system. It is intentionally split between confirmed current prospects, in-system players who may be more AHL/NHL depth than prospects, former/graduated names, and the 2026 draft watch.

## Working Definition

A player belongs in the active prospect registry if the Rangers drafted, signed, acquired, or currently control him and he is still in a development path: CHL, NCAA, Europe, AHL, ECHL, or early NHL graduation watch.

Older two-way veterans and established NHL regulars are tracked separately so the project stays complete without blurring the prospect list.
