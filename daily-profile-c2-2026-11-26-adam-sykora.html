Biography and Rangers acquisition

The Rangers selected #8 Adam Sykora 63rd overall in the second round of the 2022 NHL Draft, then signed him to a three-year entry-level contract. The Piestany, Slovakia native is a left-shot winger whose game is built on skating, pace and pressure.

Sykora's 2025-26 included 11 NHL games with New York and a 29-point AHL season with Hartford. His value starts with a relentless forecheck, defensive effort and willingness to work inside. The next development step is turning more retrievals into composed scoring chances.
