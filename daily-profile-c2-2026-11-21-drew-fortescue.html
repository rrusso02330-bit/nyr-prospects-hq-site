Biography and Rangers acquisition

The Rangers selected #6 Drew Fortescue in the third round, 90th overall, of the 2023 NHL Draft and signed him to a three-year entry-level contract on March 21, 2026. The Pearl River, New York native completed three seasons at Boston College before making his NHL debut last spring, recording two assists in nine Rangers games.

Fortescue is a defense-first left-shot blueliner who uses tight gaps, reach, skating and penalty-kill utility to handle difficult minutes. His next development step is building more confidence and variety on breakouts while maintaining the simple, matchup-driven foundation.
