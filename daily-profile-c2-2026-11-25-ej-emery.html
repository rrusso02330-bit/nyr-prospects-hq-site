Biography and Rangers acquisition

The Rangers selected #7 EJ Emery 30th overall in the first round of the 2024 NHL Draft from the U.S. National Team Development Program. He is a long, mobile defender whose game starts with skating, reach, rush defense and penalty-kill range.

After recording 3 goals, 10 assists and a +17 rating in 38 games as a North Dakota sophomore, Emery has opened his junior season with a plus-nine rating in 13 games. The next development step is making his puck touches and breakout decisions more consistently assertive under forecheck pressure.
