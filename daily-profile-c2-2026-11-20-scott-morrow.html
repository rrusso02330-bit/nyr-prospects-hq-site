Biography and Rangers acquisition

The Rangers acquired #5 Scott Morrow, a 2026 second-round pick and a conditional first-round pick from Carolina for K'Andre Miller on July 1, 2025. Morrow was originally selected by the Hurricanes in the second round, 40th overall, in the 2021 NHL Draft.

Morrow is an offensive right-shot defenseman with puck-moving skill, skating confidence and power-play upside. He looks to activate through pressure and create from the blue line; the next NHL step is managing risk and defending with consistency without losing that transition value.

On November 16, Morrow scored his first Rangers goal: a wrist shot from the point through traffic against Montreal.
